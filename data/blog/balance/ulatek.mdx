There is a very clear distinction between these talent builds that you can make, and it all depends on what your raid needs. EC will bring more stacked AoE and boss damage, KotG will bring more spread AoE.

If you look at RWF or early kills, everyone is playing 2 Balance Druids with KotG. This was before the nerfs, where eggs got reduced from 12 to 8. Nowadays with 8 eggs and the fact that you have more % damage from the raid buff, you can get away with playing 1 Balance Druid. This means that if you are going into the boss with 2, it is probably wise that one of you specs EC, and the other KotG. The later you go into the season, the more likely it is that the correct play would be 2 EC.

There are two main distinctions you can make here: whether you need damage on the intermission eggs or not. If you have 2 Balance Druids, only one !102560|Incarnation! should be pressed here, if even. On your first pull here, you should probably have both pressed, as it is very unlikely that you will manage to one-shot P3.

If it is prog and you only care about killing the boss, you should assume that your heart will live the full 20 seconds.

- **If you are EC:** only use 2 P3 CDs and place them at either 7:30 OR 8:40, and 9:44. In my opinion the more valuable !394013|Incarnation: Chosen of Elune! on prog would be the 8:40 one on the double Shrieker spawn, as you might need some damage on them. Here you can also decide to either !Starfall! the Shriekers to have a safe last platform, or !Starsurge! the boss for more boss damage if you are behind on boss damage.
- **If you are KotG:** use 3 P3 CDs as shown above. Here you are running the risk of not using your 3rd !394013|Incarnation: Chosen of Elune!, but if the boss dies before you use it, why would you care.

## Trinkets
1x Equip + Hexlord or Vial

- If you are playing Hexlord, macro it to !394013|Incarnation: Chosen of Elune!.
- If you are playing Vial + EC, the 7:35 CD is better than 8:35.

Hexlord here is very good and better than Vial in most cases, especially if you are close to P3. If you are starting prog, using Vial is better, as it helps you clear out the eggs early in progression. Best case scenario, you are using Vial until you finish P2 once, then you swap to Hexlord.

### Bear HotW

The most important one is making sure you live the Serpent's Bite overlap in P3 (7:00), the rest are free for all mostly whenever you get hit by a wave.

Another good !Heart of the Wild! would be before the breaks on 3:35, as it also lasts for the egg pickup + delivery around 3:50, which is also pretty dangerous, especially early in progression.

## Stampeding Roar
