<BossCard title="Sszorak" image="raids/the_venomous_abyss/sszorak.png">
- The common mythic strat is for everyone to not use winds. Your ability to disjoint the wind is !Wildcharge!.
- Bear the initial knock-up and bear charge boss.
- Take !Remove Corruption! for the Tempest debuff. Preferably you dodge tornadoes.
</BossCard>

<BossCard title="The Twin Fangs" image="raids/the_venomous_abyss/the_twin_fangs.png">
- !Incapacitating Roar! is used here to break orbs. !Typhoon! doesn't work.
- You can !Wildcharge! and !Flap! for distance to get over the gaps in the middle corners.
</BossCard>

<BossCard title="The Coiled Altar" image="raids/the_venomous_abyss/the_coiled_altar.png">
- !Tiger Dash! is taken here for orb movement. Accomanpied with evoker's time spiral you can cover a lot of orb timings.
- !Remove Corruption! can be taken here to dispel the random poison put on your raid.
- Cooldown timings are pretty fluid. You should use your cooldowns where your guild is struggling. Having cooldowns for all the shields in P3 isn't a bad thing.
</BossCard>

<BossCard title="Ula'tek" image="raids/the_venomous_abyss/ulatek.png">
- !Remove Corruption! can be taken here to dispel poison from your tank while your healers are doing eggs.
- !Tiger Dash! isn't bad here with how tough some of the wave line-ups are.
- Watch a vod of other boomkins for the wave movement.
</BossCard>
