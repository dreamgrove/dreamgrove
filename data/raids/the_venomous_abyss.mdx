<Encounter title="Sszorak">

The common Mythic strat is for everyone to not use the winds. Your ability to disjoint the wind is !Wild Charge!.

Bear the initial knock-up, then bear charge the boss.

### Tempest

Take !Remove Corruption! for the Tempest debuff. Preferably you dodge the tornadoes.

</Encounter>

<Encounter title="The Twin Fangs">

!Incapacitating Roar! is used here to break orbs. !Typhoon! doesn't work.

You can !Wild Charge! and !Flap! for distance to get over the gaps in the middle corners.

</Encounter>

<Encounter title="The Coiled Altar">

!Tiger Dash! is taken here for orb movement. Accompanied by an Evoker's Time Spiral you can cover a lot of orb timings.

!Remove Corruption! can be taken here to dispel the random poison put on your raid.

### Cooldowns

Cooldown timings are pretty fluid. Use your cooldowns where your guild is struggling; having cooldowns for all the shields in P3 isn't a bad thing.

</Encounter>

<Encounter title="Ula'tek">

!Remove Corruption! can be taken here to dispel poison from your tank while your healers are doing eggs.

!Tiger Dash! isn't bad here with how tough some of the wave line-ups are.

<SpecNote spec="balance">

Watch a VOD of other Boomkins for the wave movement.
